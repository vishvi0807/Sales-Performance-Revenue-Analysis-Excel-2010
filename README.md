# Sales Performance & Revenue Analysis | Excel 2010

## Project Overview

Sales Performance & Revenue Analysis is a real-world-style business analytics project developed in Microsoft Excel 2010.

The objective was to analyze sales transactions and create a management-focused reporting solution that helps stakeholders understand revenue, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, salesperson performance, geographic performance and delivery patterns.

## Business Problem

Management wants to understand:

- Overall sales and profitability
- Salesperson performance
- Customer segment contribution
- Product category performance
- State/region performance
- Monthly revenue trends
- Delivery performance

The goal is to convert raw transaction data into meaningful business insights that can support further investigation and decision-making.

## Project Objectives

1. Clean and prepare the sales data.
2. Calculate important business KPIs.
3. Analyze sales and profitability.
4. Compare salesperson performance.
5. Analyze customer segments.
6. Analyze product categories.
7. Analyze state performance.
8. Identify monthly revenue trends.
9. Analyze delivery performance.
10. Build an interactive management dashboard.

### 2. Customer Analysis

Compared:

- Consumer

Corporate customers generated the highest revenue and order volume, while Home Office showed the highest profit percentage.

### 3. Product Category Analysis

Analyzed:

- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

Technology generated the highest revenue and gross profit, while Office Supplies generated the highest unit volume.

### 4. State Analysis

Compared state-level:

- Orders
- Revenue
- Units Sold
- Average Order Value

NSW generated the highest revenue, orders and units sold.

### 5. Monthly Revenue Analysis

Analyzed monthly revenue and gross profit trends to identify high- and low-performing periods.

March recorded the highest revenue in the dataset.

Seasonality and business-cycle explanations are treated as hypotheses requiring additional data rather than confirmed causes.

### 6. Delivery Analysis

Analyzed:

- Delivery bucket
- Number of orders
- Average delivery days
- Revenue

Very Slow deliveries had the highest order volume and revenue.

This finding requires further investigation before concluding whether delivery delays are beneficial or harmful to the business.

## Key Business Insights

### Insight 1 — Salesperson Performance

Different salespeople perform strongly across different KPIs.

Therefore, salesperson performance should be evaluated using multiple KPIs rather than revenue alone.

### Insight 2 — Customer Performance

Corporate customers contribute the highest revenue and order volume.

Home Office has the highest profit percentage.

This suggests that revenue contribution and profitability should be evaluated separately.

### Insight 3 — Product Performance

Technology contributes the highest revenue and gross profit.

Office Supplies generates the highest unit volume.

This shows that sales volume and financial contribution are not necessarily the same.

### Insight 4 — Geographic Performance

NSW contributes the highest revenue and sales volume.

Further analysis by customer type and product category would help identify what is driving NSW performance.

### Insight 5 — Delivery Performance

Very Slow deliveries have the highest order volume and revenue.

Additional operational and customer data would be required to determine whether delivery time is affecting customer experience, profitability or operational efficiency.
